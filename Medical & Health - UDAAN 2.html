🌍 Study Abroad — Exams & Requirements

India

Exam: NEET-UG. Cost: low to moderate. Strong clinical exposure in many government colleges.

UAE

Exams: EmSAT / institutional tests; IELTS/TOEFL for English. Modern hospitals, growing research.

USA

Exams: MCAT + TOEFL. High fees but high research & training opportunities.

UK

Exams: UCAT/UCAS + IELTS. Many top-tier universities for medicine & allied health.
